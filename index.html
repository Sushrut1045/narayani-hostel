FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

We've answered the most common questions parents and students ask about Narayani Hostel.

What facilities are included in the hostel fee? +

The hostel fee includes furnished accommodation, three hygienic vegetarian meals per day, 24×7 high-speed WiFi, power backup, housekeeping, water purifier access, and use of study rooms. Laundry service is available at nominal cost.

What are the hostel timings and curfew? +

Students must return to the hostel by 8:00 PM daily. The main gate is locked at 9:00 PM for security. Special permission can be arranged for late coaching sessions with advance notice. Lights out is at 11:00 PM.

Are visitors allowed inside the hostel? +

Parents and guardians are welcome to visit between 10:00 AM and 6:00 PM. Visitors must register at the reception with valid ID. Only female visitors and parents are allowed inside the rooms. Male visitors including fathers are welcomed in the common sitting area only.

Is the food vegetarian? Can special dietary needs be accommodated? +

Yes, all meals served at Narayani Hostel are strictly pure vegetarian. Our kitchen follows hygienic standards with regular health inspections. Students with specific dietary requirements (medical or religious) can discuss their needs with the warden, and we do our best to accommodate them.

What is the WiFi speed and policy during study hours? +

We provide high-speed WiFi internet throughout the hostel. During mandatory study hours (6 PM – 10 PM), social media access is restricted on the network to support focused studying. Educational platforms, research, and study materials remain accessible at all times.

What are the fees and payment options? +

Fees vary based on room type (single, double, or triple sharing). Please contact us directly for current fee structures as they are updated each academic year. Payment can be made via bank transfer, UPI, or cheque. Monthly and term-wise payment options are available.

How does the hostel handle medical emergencies? +

We have a first aid kit and trained staff on premises. For medical emergencies, we have a direct tie-up with Nanded City Hospital (1.5 km away). Parents are immediately informed in any health-related situation. The warden accompanies students to the hospital when needed.

Is the mess (food) included in the fee? +

Yes, all admissions include complete dining facility with nutritious, hygienic vegetarian meals (morning tea, breakfast, lunch, evening snacks, and dinner) prepared daily in our dedicated in-house kitchen, ensuring students never have to worry about meals or outside food.
